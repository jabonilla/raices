# Raíces — Documentary Photography Candidates

Prepared 2026-10-12. Searched via image-search CLI + web verification.
Bar: "ordinary dignity" — Guatemalan/Latin American families and working people,
real kitchens, real markets, living not posing. Rejected: poverty framing,
glossy studio staging, wrong demographics, cultural mismatches, paid licenses.

**License baseline:** Every accepted photo is hosted on Pexels
(`images.pexels.com` CDN URLs, downloaded and visually inspected 2026-10-12).
All Pexels content is under the **Pexels License**: free to use for commercial
purposes, no attribution required, no sale of unaltered copies, no implied
endorsement. "Free to use" / "Free download" badges confirmed on the indexed
Pexels photo pages for the verified entries. Where the photographer name could
not be confirmed from the search index, it is marked UNCONFIRMED — check the
Pexels photo page and credit the photographer before publishing.

## Rejected

**Staged stock kitchens (glossy fakeness) — Pexels 5082217, 5082880, 5082224, 5082874:**
Black/Latino families posed in US-suburban granite-countertop kitchens, smiling
at camera while "cooking." Technically diverse casting, but the setting and
direction scream stock shoot. Destroys trust for a Guatemalan audience.

**Staged baking scenes, wrong demographic — Pexels 8798933, 8064881:**
White grandparents baking with grandchildren in styled kitchens. Wrong
demographic entirely, plus staged.

**Wrong demographics, staged interiors — Pexels 8065062, 5692994:**
White grandmother reading to grandson; East Asian family in a minimalist
concrete home. Neither is Guatemalan/Latin American.

**Staged, wrong demographic — Pexels 7492928, 5888407:**
White grandmother/granddaughter kitchen moment; white women with children in a
styled kitchen. Rejected on both axes.

**NGO/charity sites (license) — ffremittances.org, childfund.org, un-dco.org,
habitatguate.org, globein.com, flickr, arthelper.com:**
Top results for "Guatemalan family everyday life" — all charity/NGO or
commercial pages with no commercial-use license. Good subject matter, unusable
rights. Also note: NGO framing is exactly the pity register we're avoiding.

**Cultural mismatch — Pexels 12333726, 12333727, 12333729:**
Dignified portraits of an elderly craft vendor, but the beadwork and garment
style are Wixárika (Huichol, Jalisco/Nayarit, Mexico) — not Guatemalan Maya.
Using Mexican Indigenous imagery in a Guatemala-specific product is a trust
killer. Rejected on accuracy.

**Paid licenses — Getty Images, SuperStock, Alamy, Shutterstock, PIXTA,
DepositPhotos, Vecteezy Pro:**
Extensive Guatemala/Maya content (weavers, markets, vendors) but all
rights-managed or paid royalty-free. Not usable without purchase.

**Perry Grone / mana5280 Unsplash photos:**
Found only as re-uploads on third-party travel blogs (nomad.pt,
mistertravel.news, tropicanahostel.com) — could not verify the original
Unsplash photo pages or license from these copies. Rejected: never ship a
re-upload when you can't confirm the source license.
